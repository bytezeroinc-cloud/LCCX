# LowCountry Coastal Excursions — Tours, Rates & Schedule

*Prices in USD. Figures are the **base price**; a **3% card surcharge** applies at checkout.
Departure times are **typical** — confirm the current daily schedule before publishing.
Source: operator's live FareHarbor catalog. Last updated: June 2026.*

## How booking works
- **Shared** — buy individual **per-person** seats on a public departure (you share the boat).
- **Private charter** — book the **whole boat** for your group; one price by group size (no per-head charge within a band).
